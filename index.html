About Me

The person behind the work

I'm Blessing Joshua — a UI/UX designer and no-code builder based in Benin City, Nigeria. I build digital products that solve real problems for real Nigerians: a meal planner that uses actual market prices, a business tool that tracks Ajo savings, a learning app in pidgin and Yorùbá. My work sits at the intersection of thoughtful design and practical building.

I hold a Microsoft Certified: Azure AI Fundamentals credential and am currently completing dual 3MTT Nigeria fellowship tracks in UI/UX Design and Cloud Computing. I'm also an open source contributor through the Outreachy programme, curating peer-reviewed research for BugSigDB at Waldron Lab.

When I'm not designing or building, I shoot street photography on my phone and run a YouTube channel about tech and design for a Nigerian audience. I'm open to remote roles across time zones and can bring both creative and operational value to a team.
